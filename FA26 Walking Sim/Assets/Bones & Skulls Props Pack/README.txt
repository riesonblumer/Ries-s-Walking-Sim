# Low Poly Bones & Skulls Props Pack

Thank you for downloading the Low Poly Bones & Skulls Props Pack!
This package contains a collection of stylized, low-poly modular bone and skull assets designed to quickly fill out dungeon environments, catacombs, horror games, or fantasy level backgrounds. Perfect for indie games and survival projects.

## Contents
The package includes 26 unique prefabs ready to be dragged and dropped into your scene:
* Bones: Femur, Humerus, Radius, Clavicles, Spine pieces, Ribcages, and broken variants
* Skulls: Standard, Cracked, Horned, Weathered, No Jaw variants, and Lower Jaw

## Technical Details
* Number of unique models: 22
* Total number of prefabs: 26
* Polygon count: Under 900 triangles per model (highly optimized for performance)
* Textures: 1 shared color palette texture (PNG, 64x64) for maximum optimization
* Materials: Configured materials using shared color palette
* Collision: Yes (Custom Convex Mesh Colliders included on all prefabs)
* LODs: Not included
* Render Pipelines: Fully compatible with Built-in, URP, and HDRP

## How to Use
1. Open the "Prefabs" folder.
2. Drag and drop any prefab directly into your scene.
3. All models share a single texture palette, meaning you can easily modify colors by editing the provided texture file or adjusting the material.
